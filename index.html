The Syndicate

The shadow organization behind both factions' war machines — and the man who controls it.

When the Global Confederation Firmly Established Its Power

When the Global Confederation firmly established its power in 2018, it seemed that a new era of stability was just around the corner. However, not everyone benefited. The military-industrial complex was hit the hardest: the Confederation cut military budgets and defense contractors shut down production. Thousands of engineers and workers found themselves unemployed.

Some adapted to the new rules. But many refused to accept their fate.

Birth of the Syndicate

In the shadow of the glittering future the Confederation promised, a secret group of former defense industry magnates emerged. For them, global peace meant financial ruin. Thus, the Syndicate was born — an organization driven by a far greater goal than merely regaining wealth and status. They wanted war. Perpetual war.

Large sums of money, moved covertly, fueled an aggressive technological race. While the Confederation made unprecedented scientific strides, the Syndicate still outpaced them by years. The turning point came with the discovery of a new element — Uranum. By purchasing all facilities capable of mining and processing it, the Syndicate created a series of breakthrough technologies that remained hidden from the rest of the world.

It was at this time that a legendary figure arose within the group: John Doe. His cold, calculated logic, sharp insight, and commanding presence enabled him to rise to the top, with no rivals left standing.

The Situation in 2040

Twenty years have passed. A global economic crisis developed due to growing social inequality — an issue the Confederation failed to address. This was at least in part a result of the Syndicate's machinations. It secretly lobbied favorable legislation in the Senate while sponsoring Resistance movements, cultivating pockets of instability.

By 2040, the Syndicate's influence had infiltrated every layer of society — from ministerial offices to underground rebel workshops to the high command of the Confederation. Both sides of the war became dependent on its arms, data, and technology. Each faction was promised victory, but the conflict only dragged on.

Neither the Confederation nor the Resistance welcomes this uncomfortable alliance — but both must accept it, for rejecting it would give the other side a decisive technological edge.

A Secret Empire

The Syndicate blends the structure of a corporate entity with the mystery of a secret order. John Doe fiercely protects his plans, even from his closest associates, revealing only what each unit needs to know.

Agents mask their identities under codenames, and many lead double lives — as public-facing corporate managers, scientists, or officials. Their neighbors would be none the wiser that the friendly face next door is part of a secret organization — or that they themselves are working for it via shell foundations, political parties, and companies.

On the Cutting Edge of Progress

Advanced data encryption technologies enable the Syndicate to control the global information flow. While investing in various scientific fields, it particularly favors two over others: autonomous combat systems and cybernetic implants. Prototype war machines, piloted by artificial intelligence, are already appearing on the battlefield.

Rumours paint an even darker picture. Some conspiracy theories claim the war itself was fomented, at least in part, to create more injured veterans — future clients for implants. Terrifying as it is to consider, only John Doe knows how much of this is true.

The Syndicate's "Godfather"

Officially, John Doe is "first among equals." Yet everyone who encounters him knows his power is nearly absolute. He controls Uranum extraction and processing, commands assets larger than state budgets, and manipulates people so masterfully that even the strongest-willed become pawns.

With age, he's had more and more cybernetic implants replace his weakening flesh. However, make no mistake — his mind remains ruthlessly cunning and fearsomely dangerous.

Thus, the Syndicate continues to weave its web of intrigues, turning the world into their chessboard. And while some fight for freedom and justice and others — for order and progress, only John Doe truly knows what's really at stake in this war.
